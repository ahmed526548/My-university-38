### Ohm’s Law
Materials resist the flow of electric charge. This property is called resistance and is represented by R. The resistance of a uniform material depends on its cross-sectional area A, length ℓ, and resistivity ρ. Copper and aluminum have low resistivity; mica and paper have high resistivity.

The circuit element used to model resistance is the resistor.

### Passive sign convention
The current direction and voltage polarity must follow the passive sign convention:
- If current flows from higher potential to lower potential: v = iR.
- If current flows from lower potential to higher potential: v = −iR.

### Resistors
A resistor may be fixed or variable. Most resistors are fixed, meaning their resistance remains constant.

### Conductance
Conductance G is the reciprocal of resistance and measures how well an element conducts electric current. Units include mho (℧) and siemens (S).

### Nodes, branches, and loops
- A branch represents a single element such as a voltage source or resistor.
- A node is the connection point between two or more branches.
- A loop is any closed path in a circuit.
- Series elements share a single node and carry the same current.
- Parallel elements connect to the same two nodes and have the same voltage.
